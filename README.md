## The six plateau studies (from the promo carousel, Oct 12)

| # | Plateau reason | Study and finding | Where it goes in the run of show | Workbook link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | You're eating more than you think | Lichtman et al., New England Journal of Medicine, 1992. People who said they couldn't lose weight on under 1,200 calories had normal measured metabolism. They underreported food by 47% and overestimated exercise by 51%. Several were on thyroid medication and still measured normal. | Exercise 2: Map yesterday (hidden calories) | Page 3, hidden calories; Page 6, 3-day photo homework |
| 2 | The name of your diet doesn't matter | Gardner et al., JAMA, 2018 (DIETFITS). 609 adults, one year. Low-fat lost about 12 pounds, low-carb about 13. Insulin response did not predict who did better on low-carb. | Teach: The one rule. Line: "Hashimoto's changes your number. It doesn't change the rule." | Page 1, the three rules |
| 3 | Low-protein meals keep you eating | Hall et al., Cell Metabolism, 2019 (NIH). On ultra-processed food, people ate about 500 more calories a day and gained weight. Protein intake was about the same on both diets; it took 500 extra calories to get there. | Teach: The protein trap (fullness part) | Page 2, protein per meal |
| 4 | Protein is high, fiber is low | Morton et al., British Journal of Sports Medicine, 2018 (49 trials). Muscle gains level off around 1.6 g protein per kg body weight, about 110 g for a 150-pound woman. Reynolds et al., The Lancet, 2019. Biggest health benefits from fiber at 25 to 29 g a day; higher fiber went with lower body weight in trials. | Teach: The protein trap (turn). Line: "Protein is the first lever. It is not the only lever." | Page 2, numbers table and fiber wins; Page 3, protein trap box |
| 5 | Dieting without lifting | Villareal et al., New England Journal of Medicine, 2017. 160 adults with obesity, 65 and older, six months, all lost about 9% of body weight. Lean mass loss: aerobic 5%, resistance 2%, combined 3%. Say "older adults." | Teach and Exercise 3: Your movement week | Page 4 |
| 6 | Judging by Monday's scale | Orsama et al., Obesity Facts, 2014. 80 adults, about 4,600 weigh-ins. Weight highest Sunday and Monday, falls through the week. Weekend rises are normal and are offset during the week. | Teach: Weight is not fat | Page 6, how I'll read the scale |
